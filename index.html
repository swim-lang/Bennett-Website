Est 1997 · Missoula, Montana

BENNETT LAW OFFICE is a full-service transactional law firm located in the Missoula Valley, dedicated to providing a wide range of legal services. These services include the areas of tax-free vehicle registration, estate planning, commercial transactions, entity planning, liquor licensing, and real estate. The members and staff at Bennett Law Office, P.C. are committed to providing the highest degree of professional service and take pride in the consistent quality of their work.

What we help with

  • Vehicle Title Work & Registration
  • Legal Guidance Concerning Ownership, Use, & Applicable State Requirements
  • Registration Renewals & Ongoing LLC Support
  • Documentation Associated With Financed Vehicles
  • Changes in Registration & Winding Up An Entity When It Is No Longer Needed
  • Connections To Specialists For Needs Such As Transport & Insurance
